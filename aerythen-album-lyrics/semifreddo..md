## Semifreddo

I left the window open
Just in case you changed your mind
There’s a little red light blinking
Like it knows I’m wasting time

I watch the wires in the evening
Stretching somewhere out of sight
Every line goes somewhere,
But yours never crosses mine

And I think about calling
Just to hear the silence ring
Put my finger on your number
Then I take it off again

semifreddo in the sunset
Half a memory, half regret
Not cold enough to leave it
Not warm enough to forget
And the telephone keeps waiting
Like it knows you’re coming home
I keep staring at the wires
Like they might lead me to your phone

There’s a glass beside the bedside
With a mark still on the rim
I keep moving it around
But I never let it slip

The whole room is turning amber
Then the amber turns to blue
Funny how the night gets colder
When it’s waiting there for you

And I think about calling
At a quarter after two
But what would I say?
Would you want me to?
