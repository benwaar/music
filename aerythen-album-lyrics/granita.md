# GRANITA

[Verse 1]

You were already awake
when I came into the room
there was sunlight on the table
and a little afternoon

The coffee had gone cold
but you drank it anyway
you said something I can't remember
and I loved you for the way

You were laughing at the window
with your hair against the light
and the sea was turning silver
underneath the morning sky

[Chorus]

Granita in the morning
crystals melting on my tongue
something cold and something golden
something simple, something young

And I don't need to know
where the afternoon will go
if you're sitting here beside me
I already know enough

[Verse 2]

You stole a piece of orange
from the edge of my plate
I pretended to be angry
just to watch you hesitate

There was sugar on the table
there was sunlight on your sleeve
all the things I used to chase
were suddenly so easy to leave

And the room was full of nothing
but the sound of you and me
while the whole world carried on
somewhere out beyond the sea

[Chorus]

Granita in the morning
crystals melting into blue
all the things I thought I'd wanted
looked different next to you

And I don't need a telephone
I don't need a sign to know
if you're sitting here beside me
I already know I'm home

[Bridge]

I used to watch the wires
trying to follow where they went
I used to wait for someone
like a message heaven sent

Then I found you in the daylight
with your feet beneath the chair
not arriving like an answer
just already being there

And maybe love is smaller
than the stories that we make
maybe it's the second coffee
that you leave beside my plate

[Final Chorus]

Granita in the morning
melting slowly in the sun
nothing left to wait for
nothing left to overcome

The ocean keeps on moving
the whole world keeps on turning
but you're here beside me laughing
and the day has just begun

[Outro]

So leave the window open
let the morning fill the room
let the coffee lose its heat
let the sunlight find us too

No eclipse
no telephone
no shadow passing through

Just granita
and the ocean
and a morning
with you.
