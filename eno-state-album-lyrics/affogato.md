# Affogato

I came down to the water
with a thousand strangers there
everybody facing westward
everybody watching air

There were towels across the stoney beach
and voices I didn't know
no one asked me why I came
no one asked me where I'd go

And then the daylight faltered
like a signal losing sound
and every face went upward
as the shadow gathered round

Affogato in the daylight
darkness poured into the gold
something bitter, something sweet
something neither one could hold

You weren't standing next to me
I'd not met you yet
when the whole sky disappeared
I didn't feel alone

Someone laughed a little
someone whispered, here it comes
someone reached across a shoulder
someone turned to everyone

And the sea became a mirror
and the horizon lost its line
all the wires I'd been watching
seemed to vanish for a time

No telephone was ringing
no one waiting for a call
for once I wasn't looking
for a single voice at all

Affogato in the daylight
darkness poured into the gold
something bitter, something sweet
something neither one could hold

You weren't standing next to me
I'd not met you yet
when the whole sky disappeared
I didn't feel alone

Maybe this is what we're missing
all these lives that pass unseen
all these strangers carrying summers
all these places we have been

Maybe we don't need the answer
maybe we don't need to know
why a thousand separate heartbeats
can suddenly move slow

And when the light came back
I could see you all again
strangers turning into strangers
but somehow not quite the same

Affogato in the daylight
light drowning into black
for a moment there was nowhere
we could go but where we sat

You weren't standing next to me
I'd not met you yet
when the whole sky disappeared
I didn't feel alone

I left the waterfront slowly
with the evening turning blue
all those people disappearing
like the wires used to do

No telephone was waiting
no message coming through

Just the moon above the ocean
and the feeling

I belonged there too.
