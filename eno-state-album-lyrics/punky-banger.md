# Punky Banger

Verse 1

In my mind I'm a techno wizard,
mixing bangers for the crowd.
But the moment I start writing,
jazz-infused sax comes pouring out.

Spinning decks and scratching vinyl—
do they even do that now?
I still dream of K Block Disco,
sweat and noise and shouting loud.

Pre-Chorus

The moshing pit.
The flashing lights.
That feeling hit—
stayed all night.

Chorus

No drugs.
No thugs.
Too late—
you can't get in.

Turn it up.
Kick it loud.
Punky banger—
move the crowd.

Verse 2

Don't forget the synth keyboard,
or the gospel choir I adore.
Maybe next time it'll be banjo,
kicking down another door.

Strangers turning into friends,
locking lips with a random kiss.
Three minutes felt like forever-
nothing ever topped this.

Bridge

Techno. Jazz. Punk. Folk.
I can't keep it in a box.
Every song's another accident—
and somehow that still bangs.

Final Chorus

No drugs.
No thugs.
Too late—
you can't get in.

Raise your cup.
Find your grin.
Punky banger—
let us in.
